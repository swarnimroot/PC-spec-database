Spec Roster — built on real data

Picker = pure #1 (Brand · Series · Product). Year & status are multi-select toggles on the header. One year = a single roomy column; multiple years = one column each (not a unioned blob). Genuine option-lists within a year become pills. Exceptions are labelled (needs review / manual); verified is unmarked; vendor-not-published with no value is blank.

All values below are real, pulled from competitive.db — ASUS Flow Z13 and Lenovo Legion Pro 5 16.

Repeated values across years — what dedup can & can't do

Your question: drop repeated values when merging years (like CPU/GPU already do). It works for set-like fields, breaks for config-like fields. Real TUF 16 below.

WORKS — set-like fields (already deduped today)
Processor and Graphics are unordered sets of options, so a value seen twice collapses to once:
across 2026 · 2025 · 2024
ProcessorARL-HX RARL-HXRPL-HX RRPL-HXRPL-H RRPL-H
GraphicsMB2MB3 (MB2 appears in two years → shown once)
BREAKS — config-like fields (USB, Display, Dimensions…)
Dedup the USB fragments and you get conflicting counts of the same port, with no idea which year is which:
1× TB3× TB1× USB-C2× USB-C3× USB-A2× USB-A
“1× TB” and “3× TB”? Meaningless as a flat set. This field needs columns, not dedup.
THE REAL WIN — collapse fields that are identical across the selected years
Don't repeat what's the same; only expand what differs. (Shown applied in D below.)
